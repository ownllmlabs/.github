# Code of Conduct

We want OwnLLM Labs' repositories and discussions to be useful and respectful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before criticizing.
- Focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect other people's privacy.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam or unsolicited promotion

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

## Reporting

To report a problem, email [conduct-email@your-domain.com]. Reports are treated with discretion.
