# Getting help

## Questions about our open material

- Open an issue in the relevant repository. Say what you tried, what you expected and what happened, and include versions and the exact error.
- Do not post real client data, patient data, passwords or private server addresses.

## Questions about our services

Email [your-email@your-domain.com] or book a free 20-minute fit check. We will tell you whether a private setup makes sense for your team.

## Security problems

Do not report them in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Please note

Open issues are answered when we have time, and there is no guaranteed response time. Paid support for deployed systems is provided under a written support plan, and covers only what that plan states.
